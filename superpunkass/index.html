Super Punkass is an 80s-inspired 2.5D action game set in a city where the night never seems to end. After years of surviving on her own, Super Punkass makes one last move: rob Blackout City Bank, take the money, and get out.

WELCOME TO

BLACKOUT CITY.

A massive 1980s city of towering buildings, crowded streets, concrete rooftops, alleys and endless nighttime. The sun never seems to rise here.

THE CITY IS PART OF THE ESCAPE.

Explore a stylized 2.5D version of Blackout City as you move through streets, rooftops and vertical spaces. Jump from building to building, climb through the environment and find your way forward while the city closes in around you.

PRESENTATION

AN 80s ACTION MOVIE YOU CAN PLAY.

Super Punkass combines pixel-art characters with a low-poly 3D world to create a cinematic 2.5D experience inspired by the action movies and visual style of the 1980s.

CINEMATIC CUTSCENES

Key moments are presented with dramatic camera work, action-focused staging and cinematic transitions.

PIXEL + 3D

Pixel-art characters exist inside a low-poly 3D environment, combining nostalgic game visuals with a cinematic world.

CHARACTER VOICE BITS

Characters aren't fully voice acted line-by-line. Instead, expect short phrases, reactions and moments of personality throughout the experience.

SOUND & MUSIC

TURN IT UP.

An 80s-inspired retro soundtrack drives the escape, backed by punchy arcade-style sound effects and cinematic audio that keeps the action moving.
