THE GIRL

SHE RAISED HERSELF.

At 18 years old, Super Punkass has spent most of her life taking care of herself.

An orphan growing up in Blackout City, she learned early that trusting people was a luxury she couldn't afford.

Petty crime became survival. Over the years, theft, trespassing, and trouble turned her into a name people around the city knew.

She isn't trying to save anybody. She isn't trying to fix the city. She wants out.

TRUST NO ONE.

THE WORLD

BLACKOUT CITY

A massive city covered in skyscrapers, crowded streets, neon signs, and an overwhelming amount of artificial light.

The city is so bright that night and darkness have become almost impossible to separate.

Blackout City is the only geographical location in the Super Punkass universe where it always seems to be night.
