THE GIRL

RAISED BY NO ONE.

At 18 years old, Super Punkass has already spent most of her life taking care of herself. An orphan growing up in Blackout City, she learned early that nobody was coming to save her.

Over the years, petty crimes became a way of life. She became faster, tougher, more resourceful, and developed a reputation throughout the city.

But eventually, surviving isn't enough. She wants out. And she's willing to take what she needs to make it happen.

ONE CITY. ONE WAY OUT. NOTHING TO LOSE.
THE WORLD

BLACKOUT CITY

A massive metropolis buried beneath skyscrapers, crowded streets, neon signs, and an overwhelming sea of artificial light. Blackout City is the only known place in the Super Punkass universe where night seems to never end.

Super Punkass carries a pink Blaster Pistol that is biologically registered to her DNA.

Through CyberTech Chips, the Blaster can be equipped with different firing modes, changing how Super Punkass approaches combat.

RAPID SHOT Fast, continuous fire for dealing with enemies quickly.
CHARGE SHOT Build power before releasing a stronger attack.
STUN SHOT Temporarily disable enemies and create an opening.
