SUPER PUNKASS

The city is under attack, and Super Punkass is ready to fight her way out. Take control of a fearless hero armed with a pink blaster and a serious attitude as she battles through a city overrun with chaos.

Run, jump, shoot, punch, slide, climb and survive your way across the city in this cinematic 2.5D action adventure inspired by 16-bit games and classic 80s action movies.
