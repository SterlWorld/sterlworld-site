Super Punkass

BLACKOUT CITY NEVER SLEEPS.

Super Punkass is an 80s-inspired 2.5D action game set in a city where the night never seems to end. After years of surviving on her own, Super Punkass makes one last move: rob Blackout City Bank, take the money, and get out.

Gameplay

SHE'S TAKING OVER THE CITY.

Move through Blackout City, fight anything that gets in your way, and keep moving before the entire city closes in.

8-DIRECTIONAL MOVEMENT

Move freely through the world and keep control during the chaos.

JUMP & CLIMB

Cross rooftops, reach higher ground, and use the city itself to keep the escape moving.

SLIDE

Get low, move quickly, and slip through danger before it catches you.

CLOSE-QUARTERS COMBAT

Punch, shoot, and fight your way through anyone who decides to get in your path.

Cinematic Experience

AN 80s ACTION MOVIE YOU CAN PLAY.

Super Punkass blends pixel-art characters with low-poly 3D environments to create a playable 1980s action-movie experience.

CINEMATIC CUTSCENES

Story moments are framed to feel like scenes from an action movie.

PIXEL + 3D

2D pixel-art characters move through a stylized low-poly 3D world.

CHARACTER VOICE BITS

Characters have short phrases, reactions and moments spoken aloud throughout the experience.
