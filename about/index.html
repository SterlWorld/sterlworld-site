About SterlWorld

SterlWorld is an independent creative studio founded by Sterlin Griffin, built around original games, characters, worlds, and interactive experiences.

The Studio

Building Worlds With Their Own Identity

SterlWorld exists to create original entertainment properties that feel distinct, memorable, and alive. Rather than treating each project as an isolated release, the studio is building a growing library of original intellectual properties with their own characters, visual identities, stories, and gameplay experiences.

The work spans game development, interactive experiences, character-driven storytelling, and visual media — with a focus on strong creative direction and recognizable worlds.

At the center of that approach is a simple idea: every world should feel like somewhere worth coming back to.

Flagship Universe

Pizza Dales

Pizza Dales is the flagship universe of SterlWorld — a character-driven arcade world built around speed, humor, rivalry, chaos, and personality.

Its characters and settings form the foundation of a larger universe designed to expand across games and other forms of interactive entertainment. The world began with the goal of capturing the energy and personality of classic animated entertainment while building something entirely original.

The first official release, Pizza Dales: Detention Dash , brings players into the halls of Yokey Oats High, where Dale and Rocket turn an ordinary school day into a fast-moving escape through the Pizza Dales universe.

Expanding The Universe

More Worlds Are Taking Shape

SterlWorld is no longer centered around a single project. New characters, concepts, and worlds are being developed alongside the studio's existing properties.

Super Punkass is the next major world currently in development. Set within the neon-lit streets of Blackout City, the project explores a different side of the SterlWorld identity through fast-paced action, cinematic presentation, and an entirely new cast of characters.

Each new property gives SterlWorld another way to experiment with gameplay, art direction, characters, and storytelling while contributing to the larger creative ecosystem the studio is building.

Creative Direction

Originality First

SterlWorld is built around original ideas, recognizable visual identity, gameplay-first experiences, memorable characters, and worlds that can continue to grow over time.

The goal is not simply to release individual games. It is to establish original properties that can evolve, connect, and live beyond a single release.

From a small game concept to a larger universe, every project is part of the same long-term creative direction: build something original, give it an identity, and keep expanding it.
