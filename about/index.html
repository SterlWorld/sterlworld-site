About SterlWorld

SterlWorld is an independent creative studio founded by Sterlin Griffin, creating original games, characters, worlds, and entertainment experiences.

Building Worlds With Their Own Identity

SterlWorld is built around the idea that original worlds should feel like more than just games. They should have their own characters, personalities, visual identities, stories, and experiences that people can connect with.

Games are where SterlWorld is beginning, but they are not where the vision ends. The studio is building original intellectual properties with the potential to grow into different forms of entertainment over time.

Every world is approached with its own creative identity. Some may draw inspiration from a particular era, genre, or style, while still bringing something new to the experience.

Pizza Dales

Explore Pizza Dales →

Pizza Dales is SterlWorld's first original universe and the studio's first major introduction to this approach to world-building. Its colorful characters and visual style are inspired by the feeling of 1990s cartoons, bringing that nostalgic energy into an interactive world.

The first official release, Pizza Dales: Detention Dash, introduced the Pizza Dales universe to players while also marking SterlWorld's first officially released game.

Detention Dash began as an endless runner, but its role within SterlWorld goes beyond its gameplay. It was the first opportunity to take one of the studio's original worlds from an idea to a finished, shipped experience.

Expanding the Universe

SterlWorld is continuing to develop new worlds and experiences, each with its own identity and creative direction.

Super Punkass is the next major game currently in development. While Pizza Dales draws from the nostalgic feeling of a 1990s cartoon, Super Punkass takes inspiration from the atmosphere of 1980s movies and the experience of playing games in the 1990s, while bringing a modern touch to the experience.

The project represents a larger step forward for SterlWorld, moving beyond the studio's first endless runner into a fully planned action-platforming experience with story, cinematic presentation, and a 2.5D visual approach.

As SterlWorld grows, new properties will continue to explore different genres, eras, aesthetics, characters, and ways of telling stories.

More Than Games

Games are the foundation of SterlWorld's journey, but the long-term vision extends beyond interactive software.

The studio is ultimately interested in creating original entertainment properties that can exist across different mediums, including games, animation, characters, stories, and other creative experiences.

Those possibilities will grow alongside the worlds themselves. For now, SterlWorld is focused on building them one project at a time.

Originality First

SterlWorld is built around original ideas and strong creative identities. The goal is to create worlds that are recognizable, memorable, and worth exploring beyond a single release.

Nostalgia is part of that creative language, but it is not about simply recreating the past. It is about capturing the feelings, styles, and experiences that made different eras of entertainment memorable and bringing those influences into something new.

From the first game to the worlds that come after it, SterlWorld is building toward something bigger: original properties with their own identities, stories, and places in the larger creative landscape.
