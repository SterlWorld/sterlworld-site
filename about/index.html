About SterlWorld

SterlWorld is an independent creative studio founded by Sterlin Griffin. We're building original games, characters, worlds, and experiences with their own identities.

What We're Building

SterlWorld is about creating worlds that feel like they could exist beyond the screen. Games are where we're starting, but they're only the beginning.

Each project has its own style, characters, atmosphere, and personality. We're interested in creating experiences that feel familiar, nostalgic, and completely new at the same time.

Pizza Dales

EXPLORE PIZZA DALES →

Pizza Dales is the first universe created under SterlWorld. Its colorful characters and world are inspired by the feeling of 1990s cartoons — the kind of strange, memorable worlds that stick with you long after the episode ends.

Pizza Dales: Detention Dash was SterlWorld's first official release and the first step toward turning that world into something people could actually play.

PLAY DETENTION DASH →

What's Next

Super Punkass is the next major world currently in development.

Where Pizza Dales draws from the nostalgic feeling of a 1990s cartoon, Super Punkass looks toward the feeling of an 80s movie experienced through a 90s game — with a modern edge.

It's SterlWorld's first fully planned action-platformer, built around story, gameplay, cutscenes, and a 2.5D world.

More Than Games

Games are where SterlWorld begins. The bigger vision is to build original properties that can eventually reach beyond games into animation, characters, stories, and other forms of entertainment.

We're still building it.
